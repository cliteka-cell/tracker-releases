A study tracker that stays on your computer

Time, notes, books and flashcards in one place, for languages, a degree, research or anything else you are learning. No account, no cloud, and nothing leaves your computer unless you turn something on.

Download for Windows Read the guide

Free to download and use (about 45 MB). Windows 10 and 11 (64-bit); there is no Mac or Linux version yet. Windows may say “Windows protected your PC” the first time, because the program is not signed with a paid certificate: choose “More info”, then “Run anyway”.

What it does

Everything a student or self-learner tends to keep in five different places, in one program that opens in your browser and runs on your own PC.

Timer and log

Start, pause and stop a session for a course or subfield and an activity, add a past one by hand, and see your streak. Break reminders are optional.

Goals and stats

Weekly goals for each course, long-term goals with a pace to finish on time, a calendar of your days, and charts of where the time went.

Several trackers

One for a language, one for your degree, one for a hobby. Each has its own courses or topics and activities, and an overview shows them all together.

Notes that link

Formatted notes with screenshots, tied to a course or a book. Link notes with double brackets and explore how they connect in a graph.

A library

Look up a book by its ISBN or an article by its DOI, keep the cover, track the page you are on, write notes with page numbers and copy citations (BibTeX and RIS).

Optional AI, on your terms

Ask questions about your own notes and get answers that name their sources, and make flashcards. It is off until you turn it on, and it uses a model on your computer (Ollama) or your own online key, never ours.

Backups you can use

A copy is saved every day, you can restore one with a preview first, and take everything with you as one file or a zip of readable data. If the data file is ever damaged, Tracker lists the saved copies and puts one back.

Themes and comfort

Dark, light, black, forest, rose and a glassy Aero theme, or your own colours, font and text size. Text contrast is checked in every theme, everything can be done with the keyboard, and the controls are labelled for screen readers.

Updates itself, if you want

Check for a newer version when you like, or let Tracker install updates by itself the next time it starts. Only updates signed with Tracker’s own key are installed automatically.

Your data stays with you

Tracker has no account and no server of its own. Everything you log, write and save is kept in one file on your computer.

What can leave your computer, and only when you use it:

  • Book and article search sends the ISBN, DOI or words you type to Open Library, Crossref and (if you saved a key) Google Books or ISBNdb. Only when you press Search.
  • Cover pictures are loaded from the cover sites when search results show them. Turn covers off and none is loaded.
  • The AI tab is off by default. With a model on your own computer, nothing leaves it. With an online service you choose and give a key to, the text it needs to answer does go to that service.
  • Updates ask GitHub for one small file when you press Check now, or once a day if you turn that on. They carry only the program’s name and version.

There are no analytics, crash reports or advertising, and the program only answers to your own computer. Inside Tracker, Settings, Privacy lists all of this again with your own settings, and it is checked against the program’s code by a test.

Install it

  1. Download Tracker-Setup.exe (always the newest version). Older versions and checksums are on the releases page.
  2. Open it. If Windows says “Windows protected your PC”, choose “More info” and then “Run anyway”. It does not need administrator rights.
  3. Click Next and Install. Tracker opens in your browser, and a small icon near the clock opens or quits it.

Your data is kept in %LOCALAPPDATA%\Tracker\data. Installing a newer version over an old one keeps it. To check a download, each release lists the SHA-256 checksum of its Setup file.

Questions

Is it really free? Is it open source?

It is free: downloading and using Tracker costs nothing. It is not open source: the program’s code is not published.

Does it work without the internet?

Yes. The timer, log, notes, goals, stats, backups and the AI tab with a model on your own computer all work offline. Only book lookups, covers, updates and an online AI service need a connection.

Can I move to another computer?

Yes. In Settings you can take everything with you as one file or as a zip of readable data (pictures included), and restore it on the other computer. Saved keys are left out of those files on purpose.

What if something breaks?

Tracker explains problems in plain words (a full disk, a damaged data file) and keeps a log file; Settings, About shows where. If you ask for help, read the log first: a failed save can leave a piece of what you were saving in it. You can open an issue on GitHub, or write to clite.ka@gmail.com.

How do I get in touch?

Write to clite.ka@gmail.com, or open an issue on GitHub if you have a GitHub account. Questions, problems and ideas are all welcome.

What about Mac and Linux?

Not yet. The program is built for Windows 10 and 11 (64-bit) only for now.
